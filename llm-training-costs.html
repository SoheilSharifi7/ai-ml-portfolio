The Price of Training Intelligence

From raw data to deployed LLMs: process, compute, energy, and cost

Six-stage LLM development pipeline

01 · Data collection

Acquire text, code and other authorized training data.

02 · Curation

Filter, deduplicate, assess quality and privacy.

03 · Tokenization

Convert content to model-readable token sequences.

04 · Pretraining

Optimize transformer parameters through next-token prediction on accelerator clusters.

05 · Alignment & evaluation

Instruction-tune, evaluate capabilities and mitigate risks.

06 · Deployment

Serve, monitor and maintain models; inference has ongoing costs.

Four categories of training resources

Data

Tokens, acquisition, licensing, cleaning and annotation.

Computation

GPUs/TPUs, networking, storage and accelerator-hours.

Energy

Electricity and cooling, with emissions depending on grid intensity.

Time

Elapsed training time, experiments and total GPU-hours.

Classification rationale and reflection

The visual separates stages (what happens), resource categories (what is consumed), and cost outcomes (what is estimated or disclosed). These classifications make cross-model comparisons more accurate because providers disclose different metrics. I learned that large-model training cannot be reduced to parameter counts or a single price tag: data quality, hardware efficiency, energy supply and time all interact. The same discipline matters in transportation planning, where a dashboard must distinguish observed metrics, modeled estimates and operational decisions.
